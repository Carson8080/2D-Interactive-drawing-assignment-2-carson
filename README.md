# 2D-Interactive-drawing-assignment-2-carson

INSTRUCTIONS:

1. There are four buttons on your screen, they should be red, orange, yellow, and white in descending order.
2. If you click on the red button, it should swap to a white screen with a green button in the red buttons place.
3. If you click the green button, the screen will switch back to its original state.
4. If you click on the orange button, it should swap to a white screen with a blue button in the orange buttons place.
5. If you click the blue button, the screen will switch back to its original state.
6. If you click on the yellow button, it should swap to a white screen with a purple button in the yellow buttons place.
7. If you click the purple button, the screen will switch back to its original state.
8. If you click on the white button, it should swap to a white screen with a green, blue, purple, and black button in the places of all the buttons on the previous screen.
9. If you click the black button (which was once the white button), the screen will switch back to its original state.
10. You can repeat this infinitely.
